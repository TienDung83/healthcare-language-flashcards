Healthcare & Engineering Japanese Flashcards — V9

Contents: 449 Japanese vocabulary items covering mechanical design, medical devices, vaccination and infant care, hospital visits, dental and digestive examinations, diabetes and common tests, cooking, gardening, tea and coffee.

V9 quality improvements:
- Rewrote examples for the added vaccination, infant-care, hospital, cooking, gardening, and tea/coffee vocabulary so that the Japanese sentences fit the meaning and the English translations match.
- Corrected the examples for 煮る (にる): 大根をだし汁で柔らかくなるまで煮ます。 / 離乳食用のにんじんを、十分に柔らかく煮ました。
- Reviewed Sino-Vietnamese readings for the newly added themed vocabulary. Kana and loanwords are labelled instead of being forced into a Sino-Vietnamese reading.

Files: index.html, app.js, manifest.json, README.txt

To update GitHub Pages, upload/replace all files in this ZIP in your existing repository and commit the changes. Open the Pages URL in Safari and refresh. If the old version is cached, close and reopen the site or clear Safari website data.

Note: Sino-Vietnamese readings are learning aids for Kanji characters, not the Japanese pronunciation. Some mixed-script words and loanwords do not have a standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
