Healthcare & Engineering Japanese Flashcards – V6

What's new in V6:
- Each round selects 5 random words and moves through all 5 in sequence.
- New combined quiz mode: choose the correct Japanese reading, then choose the correct English meaning for each word.
- Separate English-meaning quiz mode.
- Word details include English, Vietnamese, and Hán-Việt meanings where an established equivalent is available.
- Two varied Japanese example sentences per word, with English translations. Sentence patterns vary by topic and word instead of using one repeated template.
- Custom words can include an optional Hán-Việt meaning.
- Keeps the original industry-specific vocabulary and local Library feature.

Files:
- index.html
- app.js
- manifest.json
- README.txt

GitHub Pages update:
1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. Replace index.html, app.js, manifest.json, and README.txt in your existing repository.
3. Commit and push the changes.
4. Reopen the site on iPhone Safari. If an older version appears, refresh the page or clear the cached website data.

Notes:
- Custom words are stored in the browser's localStorage on that device/browser.
- Hán-Việt is shown where a useful equivalent has been mapped. For Japanese words without a natural Hán-Việt equivalent, the app indicates that it is not applicable or not commonly used.
- Examples are generated for learning context. For formal medical or engineering documents, confirm wording against company terminology and source documents.
