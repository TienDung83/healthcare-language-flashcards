Healthcare & Engineering Japanese Flashcards — V8

Contents: Japanese vocabulary for mechanical design, medical devices, vaccination and infant care, hospital visits, dental and digestive examinations, diabetes and common tests, cooking, gardening, tea and coffee.

Files: index.html, app.js, manifest.json, README.txt

To update GitHub Pages, upload/replace all files in this ZIP in your existing repository and commit the changes. Open the Pages URL in Safari and refresh.

Each word includes Japanese reading, English, Vietnamese, per-kanji Sino-Vietnamese readings where available, and two examples. Sino-Vietnamese readings are supplied as learning aids; some kanji or loanwords do not have a standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
